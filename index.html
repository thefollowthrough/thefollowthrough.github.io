The Follow-Through

An independent tracker of EU decisions. Every law, sanction and Council act is pulled automatically from the EU's official journal (EUR-Lex) and checked against hard data from Eurostat.

Not affiliated with the European Union.

How we score

Goal progress: when a decision serves a measurable EU target, we show how far Europe has come toward that target, using official statistics.

Rollout: otherwise, we show how many of the decision's legal milestones (entry into force, deadlines) have passed.
