The Scorecard

The EU has set itself numbers to hit by 2030. We check each one against the latest official statistics from Eurostat, every day. See the charts →

  • Cut net greenhouse gas emissions by 55% by 2030
    Set by: European Climate Law, Regulation (EU) 2021/1119    68% of the way
  • Get 42.5% of Europe's energy from renewables by 2030
    Set by: Renewable Energy Directive, (EU) 2023/2413    50% of the way
  • Get 78% of 20–64-year-olds into work by 2030
    Set by: European Pillar of Social Rights Action Plan (2021), endorsed by EU leaders at the Porto Social Summit    64% of the way
  • Halve the number of people killed on EU roads by 2030
    Set by: EU Road Safety Policy Framework 2021–2030, based on the Valletta Declaration endorsed by EU transport ministers (2017)    25% of the way
  • Have 20 million ICT specialists at work in the EU by 2030
    Set by: Digital Decade Policy Programme 2030, Decision (EU) 2022/2481    14% of the way
  • Cut Europe's final energy use to 763 Mtoe by 2030
    Set by: Energy Efficiency Directive, (EU) 2023/1791    46% of the way
  • Recycle 60% of household waste by 2030
    Set by: Waste Framework Directive, as amended by Directive (EU) 2018/851    22% of the way
  • Give 80% of adults at least basic digital skills by 2030
    Set by: Digital Decade Policy Programme 2030, Decision (EU) 2022/2481    25% of the way
  • Lift 15 million people out of poverty or social exclusion by 2030
    Set by: European Pillar of Social Rights Action Plan (2021), endorsed by EU leaders at the Porto Social Summit    0% of the way
  • Farm 25% of EU farmland organically by 2030
    Set by: Farm to Fork Strategy (2020) and the EU action plan for organic production (2021)    11% of the way
