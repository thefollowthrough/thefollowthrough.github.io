The official title

Directive (EU) 2024/1500 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 May 2024 on standards for equality bodies in the field of equal treatment and equal opportunities between women and men in matters of employment and occupation, and amending Directives 2006/54/EC and 2010/41/EU

Summary

Directives (EU) 2024/1499 and 2024/1500 aim to improve the effectiveness of equality bodies across the European Union (EU) and guarantee their independence. Directive (EU) 2024/1499 amends Directives 2000/43/EC on equal treatment irrespective of racial or ethnic origin and 2004/113/EC on the equal treatment of men and women outside the employment market. Directive (EU) 2024/1500 amends Directives 2006/54/EC on gender equality in the labour market and 2010/41/EU on equal treatment between men and women in self-employment. The directives broaden the mandate of equality bodies to cover: religion or belief, disability, age and sexual orientation in the area of employment (Directive 2000/78/EC); gender in the area of social security (Directive 79/7/EEC).

Across the 27 countries

15 of 27 countries have notified national measures to the European Commission. The deadline for national laws was 19 June 2026. Not notified yet: Croatia, Cyprus, Denmark, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Slovakia.
